Questions

The bits people usually ask

Is this allowed by Google?

Yes. Trey simply makes your Google review page easy to reach and helps you write a reply. It never chooses who can leave a review, hides bad ones, or offers rewards for reviews — so it stays firmly within Google's review policies.

What's included in the free trial?

The review engine — your tap stand or key fob, unlimited AI-drafted replies, one-tap WhatsApp approvals, your monthly report, and your Trey Score so you can see exactly where your Google profile stands and what's holding it back. The profile work itself — writing your categories, services and description ready to go live, then the monthly post and quarterly photo prompts — is part of the subscription and switches on when you join. Everything in the plan above is included once you subscribe; the trial is focused on the part that needs to become a habit.

Do we still have to ask customers?

Yes — and it's the bit that matters most. A quick "would you mind tapping that?" is what turns a happy customer into a review; no stand on earth does that for you. What Trey does is make saying yes effortless — one tap, straight to your review page, no app or searching — so far more of those asks actually turn into reviews. Trey also counts taps, so if the asking tails off, you'll see it in your monthly report.

Do I need to change anything?

No dashboard and nothing to install. You connect your Google Business Profile once, pop the stand on your counter, and your review approvals come to you on WhatsApp. That's it.

What actually turns up?

Your choice: a small tap-to-review stand for the counter, or a key fob for your keyring if you work on the road. Customers tap it with their phone — or scan the QR code — and land straight on your Google review page, no app or searching needed. Got a team? Extra fobs mean everyone who meets a customer can ask.

Which review sites does it work with?

Trey focuses on Google, because for most local businesses that's where reputation is won or lost and where new customers look first. Getting your Google rating climbing is the fastest win.

What happens with a bad review?

Trey flags low-star reviews as urgent and drafts a calm, professional reply for you. Nothing is ever posted automatically — you approve every reply from your phone before it goes live.

Is there a contract?

No lock-in. It's £35 a month, cancel any time, and you start with a 14-day free trial — no card details needed to begin. If you're one of the first 20 businesses to join, it's £25 and it stays £25 for as long as you're with us.
