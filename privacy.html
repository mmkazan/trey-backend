Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 August 2026

The short version: Trey does not sell your data, or your customers' data, to anyone. Not now, not ever. We use the information described below only to run the Trey service for you.

Who this applies to

This policy covers two groups: you, the business using Trey, and your customers, whose public Google reviews Trey processes on your behalf.

What we collect

About you and your business: contact details given at onboarding (name, WhatsApp number, email), business details, Google Business Profile identifiers, reply preferences, and billing information handled directly by our payment processor.

About your reviewers: publicly available Google review content — reviewer name, star rating, review text, and date. Nothing beyond what's already visible on your public Google Business Profile.

About tap activity: when and how often your Trey Tappy Stand is tapped, to show tap-vs-organic review trends.

What we do with it

  • Generate an AI-drafted reply to each new review
  • Send you a WhatsApp alert with the review and a link to approve the reply
  • Track and show you simple stats — review counts, ratings over time, tap activity
  • Send periodic summary reports

Google user data and API services

Trey uses Google APIs — including the Google Business Profile API and the Google Places API — to read your public review data and to post the replies you approve. Trey's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Our use of information obtained through Google Maps and Places services is additionally governed by the Google Maps Platform Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy, which are incorporated into this policy by reference. We use Google user data only to provide the review-management features described above. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, transfer it to others except as needed to provide the Service, or allow humans to read it, except where you have consented, where it is necessary for security or to comply with the law, or in a limited way to operate and support the Service. You can disconnect Trey at any time by removing us as a manager on your Google Business Profile, and on request or when you close your account we delete the Google Business Profile data we hold for you.

What we don't do

  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your data or your reviewers' data to any third party
  • We do not use your business data to build marketing lists for anyone else
  • We do not share reviewer data beyond what's needed to draft and send replies on your behalf

How long we keep data

Client records are kept while you're an active subscriber, plus a reasonable period after in case you resubscribe. Reviewer data is kept for reporting and can be deleted on request. Tap logs are kept for a rolling reporting period, then aggregated or discarded.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to ask what data we hold, correct inaccuracies, request deletion, request a portable export, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact info@trey.today.

Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage and access controls.

Changes to this policy

Material changes will be communicated via WhatsApp or email before they take effect.
